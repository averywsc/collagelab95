Collage Lab 95

Collage Lab 95 is a browser-based animation studio that turns video clips and photos into hand-made, mixed-media animations. Draw frame by frame with brushes, stamps and lettering, or let the auto looks add ink, paper, paint and collage styling to every frame at once.
